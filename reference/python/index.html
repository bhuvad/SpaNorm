Python reference

Normalisation

Fit the SpaNorm model and return normalised data.

spanorm Spatially-dependent normalisation of spatial transcriptomics counts (R SpaNorm).

SVG & PCA

Spatially variable genes and GLM-based PCA.

spanorm_svg Model-based spatially variable gene (SVG) calling (R SpaNormSVG).
top_svgs The top SVGs from :func:spanorm_svg (R topSVGs).
spanorm_pca GLM-based (SpaNorm) PCA (R SpaNormPCA).

Polishing

Per-gene convergence of a fitted model.

polish_spanorm Converge each gene of a SpaNorm fit to its own optimum (R polishSpaNorm).
polish_nb Converge every gene of a shared NB fit to its own optimum (R polishNB).
nb_profile_psi Profile-ML dispersion at fixed coefficients, blocked over genes (R nbProfilePsi).
nb_mu_floor The floor on a fitted negative binomial mean, exp(-30) (R nbMuFloor).
is_polished Does the fit carry a per-gene polish (R isPolished)?

Low-level

Building blocks for custom models.

fit_nb Fit a per-gene NB GLM over an arbitrary design (R fitNB).
calculate_mu Fitted means exp(gmean + alpha @ W.T + offset) with optional row winsorisation.
filter_genes Flag genes expressed in at least prop of the spots.
fast_size_factors Library-size factors (R fastSizeFactors), written to obs["spanorm_size_factors"].

Plotting

pl.plot_spatial Plot spots at their spatial coordinates (R plotSpatial).
pl.plot_covariate Spatially plot the library-size, biology or batch effect modelled for a gene (R plotCovariate).
pl.covariate_values log2 fitted mean of one covariate, spots x genes (R normaliseMeanBio/LS/Batch at scale 1).
